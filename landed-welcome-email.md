# Landed Welcome / Next-Steps Email

Status: Draft
Last updated: 2026-07-12

## Purpose

This document defines the manual welcome email for Landed's first founding customers.

The email should be sent after a customer pays through Stripe and either creates their Landed account or needs guidance creating it.

The goal is to make the buyer feel clear, supported, and oriented without overpromising what Landed does.

## Timing

Send within one business day of confirmed payment.

## Sender

From:
- Justin / Landed
- hello@getlanded.ca

## Email Goals

The welcome email should:

- thank the customer
- confirm what they purchased
- explain what to do next
- remind them to create or confirm their Landed account
- set expectations for founder onboarding
- invite them to book or coordinate the 15-minute walkthrough
- give them a support contact
- avoid promising job placement, legal advice, resume writing, or automated coaching

## Version 1: Buyer Has Not Created Account Yet

Subject:

Welcome to Landed — next steps

Body:

Hi [First Name],

Thank you for becoming one of Landed's founding customers.

You now have access to the Landed 90-Day Recovery Roadmap, including check-ins that help update your plan as your situation changes.

Next step:

Create your Landed account here:
https://www.getlanded.ca/login

Please use the same email you used at checkout if possible. If you use a different email, just reply to this message and let me know.

Once your account is created and confirmed, complete the intake. Landed will use your answers to generate your recovery roadmap.

As part of the founding customer offer, I’ll also personally review your roadmap and help you understand the next best step. I’ll follow up to coordinate a short 15-minute walkthrough.

A quick note on scope: Landed is designed to help you stabilize, organize your next steps, and move through your job-search recovery with a clearer plan. It does not provide legal advice, job placement, resume writing, or guaranteed employment outcomes.

If anything feels confusing or does not work as expected, reply directly to this email.

Best,
Justin
Founder, Landed
https://www.getlanded.ca

Welcome to Landed — your roadmap is ready

Justin

## Founder Send Checklist

Before sending:

- Confirm Stripe payment exists.
- Confirm payment amount is $4.99 CAD.
- Confirm buyer email from Stripe.
- Check whether Supabase account exists.
- Check whether email is confirmed.
- Choose Version 1 if account is not created yet.
- Choose Version 2 if account already exists.
- Replace [First Name].
- If Stripe email and Landed account email differ, mention both are linked manually.
- Send from hello@getlanded.ca.
- Record welcome email sent date in the customer tracker.

After sending:

- Watch for reply.
- Confirm customer can access the app.
- Confirm intake is completed.
- Confirm roadmap generated.
- Coordinate 15-minute walkthrough.
